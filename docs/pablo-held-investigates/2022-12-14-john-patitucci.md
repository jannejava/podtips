# John Patitucci

[Pablo Held Investigates](https://pabloheldinvestigates.com/john-patitucci/), 2022-12-14

## Music

- **Inside Out** (Chick Corea Elektric Band, 1990, album)  
  Patitucci calls it the most adventurous Elektric Band record, like Bartók meeting the band. The demos baffled the band.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Inside%20Out%20Chick%20Corea%20Elektric%20Band) · 17:19

- **Time Warp** (Chick Corea, 1995, album)  
  Patitucci's favourite Chick Corea band, with Bob Berg and Gary Novak. The sessions also produced standards, including a great arrangement of That Old Feeling.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Time%20Warp%20Chick%20Corea) · 17:47

- **Heart of the Bass** (John Patitucci, 1992, album)  
  Patitucci's dream project with orchestra, showing his classical side. It was the first record on Chick Corea's Stretch Records.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Heart%20of%20the%20Bass%20John%20Patitucci) · 20:22

- **Phantom Navigator** (Wayne Shorter, 1987, album)  
  Pablo loves this album. Patitucci says the music was deep and brilliant live, but the electronic production did not do it justice. Songs discussed: Forbidden Planet (24:30).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Phantom%20Navigator%20Wayne%20Shorter) · 24:22

- **High Life** (Wayne Shorter, 1995, album)  
  Patitucci praises the bass lines Shorter wrote for it. The quartet later played Children of the Night from it in a new arrangement.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/High%20Life%20Wayne%20Shorter) · 26:56

- **Motherland** (Danilo Pérez, 2000, album)  
  Patitucci, Pérez and Brian Blade played together here before joining Shorter. He says they already had something special.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Motherland%20Danilo%20P%C3%A9rez) · 33:26

- **Footprints Live!** (Wayne Shorter, 2002, album)  
  Pablo heard Atlantis here first and played it repeatedly. It sounds improvised, though much of it is written. Songs discussed: Atlantis (36:25).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Footprints%20Live%21%20Wayne%20Shorter) · 36:25

- **Directions in Music** (Herbie Hancock, 2002, album)  
  One of Pablo's favourite albums, recorded live at Massey Hall with Michael Brecker and Roy Hargrove. Patitucci praises Hancock's comping and reharmonizing. Songs discussed: Naima (59:23), So What (1:01:19), The Poet (1:03:12).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Directions%20in%20Music%20Herbie%20Hancock) · 58:02

- **Line by Line** (John Patitucci, album)  
  Patitucci's earlier record with Adam Rogers on guitar, with Chris Potter and Brian Blade. He calls it a fun record.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Line%20by%20Line%20John%20Patitucci) · 1:04:28

- **Remembrance** (John Patitucci, 2009, album)  
  Pablo says it is one of his favourites. It is a trio record with Joe Lovano and Brian Blade. Songs discussed: Scenes of an Opera (2:21:41), with Sachi Patitucci's cello solo.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Remembrance%20John%20Patitucci) · 1:05:14

- **Play Domenico Scarlatti** (Enrico Pieranunzi, album)  
  Patitucci recommends it to Pablo. Pieranunzi plays Scarlatti sonatas as written, then plays meditations on each. Patitucci calls it totally killing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Play%20Domenico%20Scarlatti%20Enrico%20Pieranunzi) · 1:52:13

- **The Soul of the Bass** (John Patitucci, album)  
  Patitucci's solo bass album, recorded at the Bunker studio. Pablo's favourite song on it is Seeds of Change (1:58:02), which comes back as a reprise with drums.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Soul%20of%20the%20Bass%20John%20Patitucci) · 1:58:02

- **Sketchbook** (John Patitucci, 1990, album)  
  Patitucci's record with Michael Brecker. It has his first layered low-string clusters. Songs discussed: Two Worlds (2:24:02).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Sketchbook%20John%20Patitucci) · 2:24:02

- **One More Angel** (John Patitucci, 1997, album)  
  A chamber-jazz record with Paul Motian, Alan Pasqua and Chris Potter. Pablo loves it. Patitucci praises Motian's strong inner pulse. Songs discussed: Quasimodo (2:27:02).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/One%20More%20Angel%20John%20Patitucci) · 2:24:42
