# Larry Goldings 2

[Pablo Held Investigates](https://pabloheldinvestigates.com/larry-goldings-again/), 2021-11-01

## Music

- **Hand Jive** (John Scofield, 1994, album)  
  Larry Goldings calls it an important record for him. His family played it in the car.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Hand%20Jive%20John%20Scofield) · 9:05

- **Switched-On Brandenburgs** (Wendy Carlos, 1979, album)  
  Bach's Brandenburg Concertos played on Moog. They praise how expressive it is, and say it shows the quality of Bach's music.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Switched-On%20Brandenburgs%20Wendy%20Carlos) · 13:00

- **Rachmaninoff Piano Concertos** (Vladimir Ashkenazy, album)  
  Larry Goldings has vivid memories of this record from his childhood. He loves the orchestration and the French horns.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Rachmaninoff%20Piano%20Concertos%20Vladimir%20Ashkenazy) · 16:20

- **Goldberg Variations** (Glenn Gould, album)  
  Larry Goldings loves both the early and the late Gould recordings. They also talk about Gould's heavy editing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Goldberg%20Variations%20Glenn%20Gould) · 29:00

- **Shostakovich: Preludes and Fugues** (Keith Jarrett, 1991, album)  
  Larry Goldings says Jarrett plays them great and puts it all together well.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Shostakovich%3A%20Preludes%20and%20Fugues%20Keith%20Jarrett) · 57:09

- **Scintillation** (Carlos Salcedo, song)  
  A harp piece. Pablo Held has listened to it on repeat for months. They listen to it together, and he plays his favourite chords from it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Scintillation%20Carlos%20Salcedo) · 1:09:17

- **Sonatas and Interludes** (John Cage, 1948, album)  
  Pablo Held plays the first piece for prepared piano. He likes the idea of writing for an instrument you have remade.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Sonatas%20and%20Interludes%20John%20Cage) · 1:15:42

- **The Complete Vee-Jay Recordings** (Billy Preston, album)  
  Includes a gospel record Preston made at 20, with organ and piano overdubs. Larry Goldings says it was a turning point for him.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Complete%20Vee-Jay%20Recordings%20Billy%20Preston) · 2:01:44

- **Notes with Attachments** (Blake Mills, 2021, album)  
  Larry Goldings played on it, and he describes how Blake Mills works with sound and reshapes ideas. He mentions a beautiful free-flowing ballad on it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Notes%20with%20Attachments%20Blake%20Mills) · 2:11:17

- **The Complete "Is" Sessions** (Chick Corea, album)  
  Larry Goldings names it as his favourite Chick Corea record. The band is Dave Holland, Woody Shaw and others.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Complete%20%22Is%22%20Sessions%20Chick%20Corea) · 2:26:02

- **Lush Life** (Chick Corea, song)  
  A solo piano version from a later Concord record. Corea uses a moving ostinato bass. Larry Goldings calls it brilliant and says it is worth hearing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Lush%20Life%20Chick%20Corea) · 2:27:38

- **Autumn Leaves** (Chick Corea and Bobby McFerrin, song)  
  A funny duo version. Pablo Held loved how Corea plays behind the melody.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Autumn%20Leaves%20Chick%20Corea%20and%20Bobby%20McFerrin) · 2:29:49

- **Filles de Kilimanjaro** (Miles Davis, 1968, album)  
  Larry Goldings loves Chick Corea on some of the tracks.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Filles%20de%20Kilimanjaro%20Miles%20Davis) · 2:34:11

- **Light as a Feather** (Return to Forever, 1973, album)  
  Larry Goldings listened to it a lot and loved Corea's Rhodes playing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Light%20as%20a%20Feather%20Return%20to%20Forever) · 2:34:46

- **Soft Space** (Jeff Lorber, song)  
  Larry Goldings remembers an amazing synth solo by Chick Corea on this track.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Soft%20Space%20Jeff%20Lorber) · 2:35:01
