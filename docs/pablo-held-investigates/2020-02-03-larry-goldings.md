# Larry Goldings

[Pablo Held Investigates](https://pabloheldinvestigates.com/larry-goldings-podcast/), 2020-02-03

## Music

- **Hand Jive** (John Scofield, 1994, album)  
  Scofield record with Larry Goldings on organ and piano. Pablo Held calls it a masterpiece and played along with it as a kid.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Hand%20Jive%20John%20Scofield) · 1:09

- **Intimacy of the Blues** (Larry Goldings, 1991, album)  
  Goldings' own record. Pablo Held listened to it a lot while growing up.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Intimacy%20of%20the%20Blues%20Larry%20Goldings) · 1:33

- **Groove Elation** (John Scofield, 1995, album)  
  Scofield record with Goldings on organ. Pablo Held listened to it a lot.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Groove%20Elation%20John%20Scofield) · 1:37

- **Time Is of the Essence** (Michael Brecker, 1999, album)  
  Brecker record with Goldings on organ and three drummers: Elvin Jones, Bill Stewart and Jeff "Tain" Watts. Goldings talks about the sessions with warmth.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Time%20Is%20of%20the%20Essence%20Michael%20Brecker) · 1:40

- **Sweet Science** (Larry Goldings, 1996, album)  
  Pablo Held's favorite Goldings trio record, with tracks like Asimov, This Guy's in Love with You and Spring Is Here. Goldings explains how the jam-based tracks were edited and overdubbed.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Sweet%20Science%20Larry%20Goldings) · 1:47

- **Awareness** (Larry Goldings, 2001, album)  
  Piano trio record with Paul Motian and Larry Grenadier, including Sticky Mac and Strays. Pablo Held counts it among his top five piano trio records.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Awareness%20Larry%20Goldings) · 2:20
