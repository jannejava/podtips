# Larry Goldings

[Pablo Held Investigates](https://pabloheldinvestigates.com/larry-goldings-podcast/), 2020-02-03

## Music

- **Hand Jive** (John Scofield, 1994, album)  
  Pablo Held calls it Scofield's masterpiece. He played along with it as a kid, and Larry Goldings' organ and piano work on it made a deep impression.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Hand%20Jive%20John%20Scofield) · 1:09

- **Intimacy of the Blues** (Larry Goldings, 1991, album)  
  One of the Goldings records Pablo Held listened to a lot after his parents became fans.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Intimacy%20of%20the%20Blues%20Larry%20Goldings) · 1:33

- **Groove Elation** (John Scofield, 1995, album)  
  A Scofield record with Goldings on organ that Pablo Held listened to a lot in his youth.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Groove%20Elation%20John%20Scofield) · 1:37

- **Time Is of the Essence** (Michael Brecker, 1999, album)  
  Brecker's organ-trio record with Goldings, Pat Metheny and three drummers (Elvin Jones, Bill Stewart, Jeff "Tain" Watts). Goldings recalls the sessions warmly, including Sound Off (1:08:39) and Renaissance Man (1:09:43).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Time%20Is%20of%20the%20Essence%20Michael%20Brecker) · 1:03:49

- **Sweet Science** (Larry Goldings Trio, 1999, album)  
  Pablo Held's favorite Goldings trio record, built largely on free jams that were edited afterwards. Songs discussed: Asimov (1:02:35), This Guy's in Love With You (55:21), Spring Is Here (1:03:35), Come In and Pray (54:06), Lookout (54:44), Gnomesville (54:48), Solid Jack (55:04).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Sweet%20Science%20Larry%20Goldings%20Trio) · 52:40

- **Awareness** (Larry Goldings, Paul Motian, Larry Grenadier, 1997, album)  
  A piano trio record with Paul Motian that Pablo Held counts among his top five piano trio albums. Goldings tells how it came together and Motian's spontaneous approach. Songs discussed: Strays (3:15), Sticky Mac (2:20), You Do Something to Me (41:31).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Awareness%20Larry%20Goldings%2C%20Paul%20Motian%2C%20Larry%20Grenadier) · 36:20

- **Long Story Short** (Larry Goldings, album)  
  Pablo Held says he loves this Goldings record, which holds earlier jam material that Goldings reused on Sweet Science.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Long%20Story%20Short%20Larry%20Goldings) · 58:11
