# Anders Jormin

[Pablo Held Investigates](https://pabloheldinvestigates.com/anders-jormin/), 2024-09-18

## Music

- **Billy Boy** (Red Garland Trio, 1958, song)  
  Trio track on Miles Davis's Milestones album, with solos by Paul Chambers and fours by Philly Joe Jones. Jormin heard it more than any other jazz piece as a child and still loves it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Billy%20Boy%20Red%20Garland%20Trio) · 18:33

- **Trio '64** (Bill Evans Trio, 1964, album)  
  Gary Peacock plays daring, adventurous bass on this album. Jormin calls him one of his main influences.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Trio%20%2764%20Bill%20Evans%20Trio) · 31:07

- **Jaco Pastorius** (Jaco Pastorius, 1976, album)  
  Jaco's debut solo album, opening with the track Donna Lee. It was probably the first Jaco music Jormin heard, and he admires Jaco's melodic playing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Jaco%20Pastorius%20Jaco%20Pastorius) · 34:00

- **Closeness** (Charlie Haden, 1976, album)  
  Duo recordings by Charlie Haden with Keith Jarrett, Ornette Coleman, Alice Coltrane and Paul Motian. Jormin calls it a great, really good album.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Closeness%20Charlie%20Haden) · 35:35

- **Forward Winds** (Anders Jormin, album)  
  Jormin's newer solo bass album on ECM, with small horn miniatures added at Manfred Eicher's suggestion. Pablo Held really likes it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Forward%20Winds%20Anders%20Jormin) · 50:26

- **Everything I Love** (John Taylor, Peter Erskine Trio, song)  
  Jormin recommends this version with Peter Erskine and Palle Danielsson as the place to start with John Taylor. He praises Taylor's rhythm and his bell-like touch.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Everything%20I%20Love%20John%20Taylor%2C%20Peter%20Erskine%20Trio) · 1:31:56

- **Symphony of Birds** (Anders Jormin, album)  
  Jormin's symphony for orchestra and choir, commissioned by the Wrocław symphony orchestra and recorded at the general rehearsal. Pablo Held loves the album, especially its lyrical, slow-movement feel.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Symphony%20of%20Birds%20Anders%20Jormin) · 1:41:56

- **Ama** (Anders Jormin, album)  
  Choir album that grew out of the symphony project. It includes a version of Ascension of Larks and the song M, inspired by mean tone tuning.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Ama%20Anders%20Jormin) · 1:52:23

- **Songs in Mean Tone** (Anders Jormin, album)  
  Album with a church organ player and a folk flute player, on an organ tuned in mean tone. Pablo Held loves it and Jormin tells listeners to look it up.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Songs%20in%20Mean%20Tone%20Anders%20Jormin) · 1:58:33
