# Anders Jormin

[Pablo Held Investigates](https://pabloheldinvestigates.com/anders-jormin/), 2024-09-18

## Music

- **Billy Boy** (Red Garland Trio, 1958, song)  
  Trio-only track tied to Miles Davis's Milestones sessions. Jormin heard it more than any other jazz piece as a child, knows it by heart, and still finds it great and swinging.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Billy%20Boy%20Red%20Garland%20Trio) · 18:39

- **Trio '64** (Bill Evans, 1964, album)  
  Jormin names it as the Gary Peacock album that comes to mind first. He calls Peacock's bass playing daring and a main influence.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Trio%20%2764%20Bill%20Evans) · 31:07

- **Jaco Pastorius** (Jaco Pastorius, 1976, album)  
  Jormin's first Jaco record, which he heard as a young player. Jaco's melodic phrasing was a big influence. Donna Lee (34:05).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Jaco%20Pastorius%20Jaco%20Pastorius) · 34:00

- **Closeness** (Charlie Haden, 1976, album)  
  Haden's duo album with Keith Jarrett, Ornette Coleman, Alice Coltrane and Paul Motian. Jormin calls it a great, really good album.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Closeness%20Charlie%20Haden) · 35:53

- **Forward Winds** (Anders Jormin, album)  
  Jormin's recent ECM solo bass album with small horn miniatures that Manfred Eicher suggested. Pablo Held loves his two solo albums, and Jormin is happy with how the miniatures turned out.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Forward%20Winds%20Anders%20Jormin) · 50:19

- **Everything I Love** (John Taylor with the Peter Erskine Trio, song)  
  Jormin's tip for where to start with John Taylor, with Palle Danielsson on bass. He praises Taylor's rhythmic excellence and bell-like touch.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Everything%20I%20Love%20John%20Taylor%20with%20the%20Peter%20Erskine%20Trio) · 1:31:56

- **Symphony of Birds** (Anders Jormin, album)  
  Jormin's symphony for the Wrocław orchestra, with titles taken from names for groups of birds. Held loves the album, which was recorded at the general rehearsal.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Symphony%20of%20Birds%20Anders%20Jormin) · 1:41:56

- **Ama** (Anders Jormin, album)  
  Jormin's choir album, made after he met the choir during the symphony. Ascension of Larks (1:51:37), M (1:55:50).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Ama%20Anders%20Jormin) · 1:51:37

- **Songs in Mean Tone** (Anders Jormin, album)  
  Album with a church organ player and a folk flute player, on an organ tuned in mean tone. Held loves it, and Jormin says it is very good.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Songs%20in%20Mean%20Tone%20Anders%20Jormin) · 1:58:38
