# Fresh Spin Fridays - January 2026

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/fmf_jan), 2026-01-31

## Music

- **Why Don't You** (Sam Fribush, Corey Fonville, Charlie Hunter, 2026, song)  
  A cover of a Cleo Sol song, from a five-song EP by an organ, drums and guitar trio. Adam opens the episode with it and calls the trio incredible.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Why%20Don%27t%20You%20Sam%20Fribush%2C%20Corey%20Fonville%2C%20Charlie%20Hunter) · 0:35

- **Scenes From Above** (Julian Lage, 2026, album)  
  New Blue Note album, released January 23rd. Talking Drum (2:50) features John Medeski, Jorge Roeder and Kenny Wollesen.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Scenes%20From%20Above%20Julian%20Lage) · 2:50

- **Out of the Blue** (Winderman, Colman, Kimock, 2026, album)  
  Self-released album with a cover of Aphex Twin's Flim (4:52). Adam calls it a beautiful sounding album and says you can find it on Bandcamp.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Out%20of%20the%20Blue%20Winderman%2C%20Colman%2C%20Kimock) · 4:36

- **Motion II** (Out of Into, 2026, album)  
  Second album from the Blue Note all-star group. Adam hears a new Blue Note sound in Nacho Supreme (6:53).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Motion%20II%20Out%20of%20Into) · 6:28

- **Gospel Music** (Joel Ross, 2026, album)  
  Blue Note album about Ross's faith and church upbringing, released January 30th. Adam calls it gorgeous and praises Wisdom Is Eternal (For Barry Harris) (8:51).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Gospel%20Music%20Joel%20Ross) · 8:25

- **Indigo** (Nabou, 2026, album)  
  Album by a Belgian trombonist on Edition Records. Adam finds Unpersuadable Extern (10:37) very interesting and very good.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Indigo%20Nabou) · 10:37

- **Synergy** (Vladko, 2026, album)  
  Album by a Berlin trumpeter, released January 16th. Adam says Free Walk (12:06) is a great way to start your day.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Synergy%20Vladko) · 12:06

- **Fireball** (John Ellis & Double Wide, 2026, album)  
  New album on Sunnyside from a long-running group that Adam says is always fun. The title track Fireball (14:12) has a New Orleans feel.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Fireball%20John%20Ellis%20%26%20Double%20Wide) · 13:37

- **Multidirectional** (Billy Hart, 2025, album)  
  Live album on Smoke Sessions Records with Mark Turner, Ethan Iverson and Ben Street. Adam highly recommends it and discusses Giant Steps (15:48).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Multidirectional%20Billy%20Hart) · 15:31

- **By All Means** (Aaron Parks, 2025, album)  
  Blue Note album from November 2025 with Billy Hart and Ben Street. Adam names Parks one of his favorite pianists and discusses Parks Lope (18:53).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/By%20All%20Means%20Aaron%20Parks) · 18:32
