# The Best New Jazz of 2025

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/jazz-2025), 2025-12-29

## Music

- **Belonging** (Branford Marsalis Quartet, 2025, album)  
  Blue Note debut of the quartet, a reinterpretation of Keith Jarrett's 1974 album with the same track order. Songs discussed: Spiral Dance (1:12). The host says to hear the whole album.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Belonging%20Branford%20Marsalis%20Quartet) · 1:12

- **Songbook** (Kenny Barron, 2025, album)  
  13 original pieces with lyrics by Janice Jarrett. Songs discussed: Minor Blues Redux (3:05). The host praises how well the 82-year-old Barron plays.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Songbook%20Kenny%20Barron) · 3:05

- **Words Fall Short** (Joshua Redman, 2025, album)  
  Blue Note album with a new band of musicians in their twenties. Songs discussed: Icarus (5:08). The host loves when a master hires young, fiery players.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Words%20Fall%20Short%20Joshua%20Redman) · 5:08

- **Gillian Margot and Geoffrey Keezer** (Gillian Margot, Geoffrey Keezer, 2025, album)  
  Self-titled vocal and piano duo album. Songs discussed: Thou Swell (7:35). The host saw them live and calls the album worth hearing all the way through.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Gillian%20Margot%20and%20Geoffrey%20Keezer%20Gillian%20Margot%2C%20Geoffrey%20Keezer) · 7:35

- **Southern Nights** (Sullivan Fortner, 2025, album)  
  Trio album recorded with no rehearsals, retakes or isolation. Songs discussed: Southern Nights (9:46). The host calls it a great album with many unexpected twists.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Southern%20Nights%20Sullivan%20Fortner) · 9:46

- **Oh, Snap** (Cécile McLorin Salvant, 2025, album)  
  Album with folk and electronic touches, with Sullivan Fortner on piano. Songs discussed: Anything but Now (13:02). The host calls it a revelation and a great album all the way through.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Oh%2C%20Snap%20C%C3%A9cile%20McLorin%20Salvant) · 13:02

- **Everything Means Nothing to Me** (Brad Mehldau, 2025, song)  
  Mehldau's version of an Elliott Smith song, from his album of Elliott Smith songs. The host feared it would not work, but says he nails it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Everything%20Means%20Nothing%20to%20Me%20Brad%20Mehldau) · 17:35

- **Keys to the City, Volume One** (Robert Glasper, 2025, album)  
  Live album from Glasper's Rob Tober residency at the Blue Note. Songs discussed: Over (feat. Yebba) (22:46). The host says it feels like being in the room.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Keys%20to%20the%20City%2C%20Volume%20One%20Robert%20Glasper) · 22:46

- **About Ghosts** (Mary Halvorson, 2025, album)  
  One of the most acclaimed albums of the year, played by the Amaryllis Sextet. Songs discussed: Carved From (24:47). The host likes how she plays with ideas of what jazz and guitar are.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/About%20Ghosts%20Mary%20Halvorson) · 24:47

- **Without Much Ado, Volume One** (Christian McBride, 2025, album)  
  Album with a 17-piece big band. Songs discussed: Old Folks (26:55), with Samara Joy. The host calls it fantastic and rare for a big band album.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Without%20Much%20Ado%2C%20Volume%20One%20Christian%20McBride) · 26:55

- **Trilogy 3** (Chick Corea, Christian McBride, Brian Blade, 2025, album)  
  Live trio recording from early 2020, released after Corea's death. Songs discussed: Windows (29:38) and Humpty Dumpty (32:20). The host calls the playing unbelievable and a real treat.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Trilogy%203%20Chick%20Corea%2C%20Christian%20McBride%2C%20Brian%20Blade) · 29:38

- **Elemental** (Dee Dee Bridgewater, Bill Charlap, 2025, album)  
  Vocal and piano duo album on Mack Avenue. Songs discussed: Mood Indigo (33:28). The host calls the pairing really incredible.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Elemental%20Dee%20Dee%20Bridgewater%2C%20Bill%20Charlap) · 33:28
