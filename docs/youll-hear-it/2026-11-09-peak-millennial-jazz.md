# Peak Millennial Jazz?

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/do-we-hate), 2026-11-09

## Music

- **Lingus** (Snarky Puppy, 2014, song)  
  Snarky Puppy's We Live Here session video, with a famous Cory Henry synth solo. The hosts call it one of the most iconic solos of the 21st century and say the video started a whole genre of studio session videos.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Lingus%20Snarky%20Puppy) · 3:19

- **Cosmic Sans** (Cory Wong, song)  
  A live video from Madison, Wisconsin. The hosts call it feel-good, fun funk with a great look.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Cosmic%20Sans%20Cory%20Wong) · 20:28

- **Them Changes** (Thundercat, 2015, song)  
  Thundercat's early hit, with a throwback feel and a memorable video. It is where one host first got to know him.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Them%20Changes%20Thundercat) · 24:36

- **What's the Use?** (Mac Miller feat. Thundercat, 2018, song)  
  Mac Miller's Tiny Desk performance with Thundercat on bass. The hosts enjoy it and praise Thundercat's bass playing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/What%27s%20the%20Use%3F%20Mac%20Miller%20feat.%20Thundercat) · 26:38

- **All Blues** (Butcher Brown & Nicholas Payton, song)  
  An updated version of the Miles Davis tune from Kind of Blue. The hosts like the cinematography, the look and Payton's Miles-inspired playing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/All%20Blues%20Butcher%20Brown%20%26%20Nicholas%20Payton) · 28:46
