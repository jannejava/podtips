# Sunlight - Herbie Hancock

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/herbie-sunlight), 2026-11-16

## Music

- **Sunlight** (Herbie Hancock, 1978, album)  
  Disco, funk and jazz album where Herbie sings lead for the first time through a vocoder. The hosts call it a masterpiece. Songs discussed: I Thought It Was You (3:10), Come Running to Me (16:42), Sunlight (37:43), No Means Yes (45:48), Good Question (52:01).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Sunlight%20Herbie%20Hancock) · 3:10

- **An Evening with Herbie Hancock and Chick Corea: In Concert** (Herbie Hancock and Chick Corea, 1978, album)  
  Double album of two acoustic pianos. Adam calls it wonderful and a great album, and says they should cover it sometime.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/An%20Evening%20with%20Herbie%20Hancock%20and%20Chick%20Corea%3A%20In%20Concert%20Herbie%20Hancock%20and%20Chick%20Corea) · 55:47

- **This Year's Model** (Elvis Costello, 1978, album)  
  Called a great album, though the hosts rate Sunlight higher.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/This%20Year%27s%20Model%20Elvis%20Costello) · 1:00:08

- **One Nation Under a Groove** (Funkadelic, 1978, album)  
  Called a great record that comes close to Sunlight, but they still prefer Sunlight.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/One%20Nation%20Under%20a%20Groove%20Funkadelic) · 1:00:19

- **The Best of Earth, Wind & Fire Vol. 1** (Earth, Wind & Fire, 1978, album)  
  Compilation that includes September. The hosts call it an incredible best-of and rate it above Sunlight.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Best%20of%20Earth%2C%20Wind%20%26%20Fire%20Vol.%201%20Earth%2C%20Wind%20%26%20Fire) · 1:00:42

- **For You** (Prince, 1978, album)  
  Called incredible. Peter prefers it to Sunlight.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/For%20You%20Prince) · 1:00:55
