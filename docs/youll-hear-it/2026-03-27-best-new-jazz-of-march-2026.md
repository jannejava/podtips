# Best New Jazz Of March 2026

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/fsf_mar), 2026-03-27

## Music

- **Orchestra** (Flea, 2026, album)  
  Flea's jazz album, honoring his roots as a trumpeter. It has originals and covers, and the hosts find it interesting. Songs discussed: Traffic Lights with Thom Yorke (0:53).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Orchestra%20Flea) · 0:18

- **Twio Volume 2** (Walter Smith III, 2026, album)  
  A tenor saxophone trio album with Joe Sanders and Kendrick Scott, plus guests Branford Marsalis and Ron Carter. The hosts praise the lyrical playing and the humor. Songs discussed: My Ideal (2:55).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Twio%20Volume%202%20Walter%20Smith%20III) · 2:34

- **Around the World with You** (Ulysses Owens Jr. and Generation Y, 2026, album)  
  A swinging album on Cellar Music. The hosts praise the rhythm section. Songs discussed: Prodigal Son (4:39).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Around%20the%20World%20with%20You%20Ulysses%20Owens%20Jr.%20and%20Generation%20Y) · 4:39

- **Gene** (Yebba, 2026, album)  
  Yebba's second studio album on RCA Records. The hosts love her clear melodic style and harmonic sophistication. Songs discussed: Earth, Wind in California (7:08).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Gene%20Yebba) · 6:34

- **Alive in Philadelphia, Volume 1 at Chris's Jazz Cafe** (Alexander Claffey, 2026, album)  
  A live album with Seamus Blake, Jaleel Shaw, Kevin Hays and Bill Stewart. The hosts call it a delightful experience. Songs discussed: She (9:58).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Alive%20in%20Philadelphia%2C%20Volume%201%20at%20Chris%27s%20Jazz%20Cafe%20Alexander%20Claffey) · 9:11

- **Pattern Master** (Mark Turner, 2026, album)  
  An ECM album with a trumpet and no chordal instruments. The hosts say it sounds great and praise the cover art. Songs discussed: Super Sister (12:26).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Pattern%20Master%20Mark%20Turner) · 12:18

- **Barcode** (Ben Wendel, 2026, album)  
  A tenor saxophone album with several vibraphone players. The hosts like the contrast between percussive mallets and the soulful sax. Songs discussed: Clouds (14:34).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Barcode%20Ben%20Wendel) · 14:23

- **Figments** (Ella Grace, 2026, album)  
  A debut album by a trumpeter from Chicago. The hosts find the flugelhorn layered with voice beautiful. Songs discussed: Butterflies Watching (16:52).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Figments%20Ella%20Grace) · 16:46

- **Live at the Village Vanguard, Volume 1** (Immanuel Wilkins, 2026, album)  
  A live album on Blue Note with Micah Thomas, Rick Rosato and Kweku Sumbry. The hosts cannot wait to hear all of it. Songs discussed: Sharonam Live (19:30).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Live%20at%20the%20Village%20Vanguard%2C%20Volume%201%20Immanuel%20Wilkins) · 19:11

- **In My Dreams** (Bill Frisell, 2026, album)  
  A Blue Note album released at the end of February. The hosts added it to this show. Songs discussed: Isfahan (22:33), from Duke Ellington's Far East Suite.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/In%20My%20Dreams%20Bill%20Frisell) · 22:13
