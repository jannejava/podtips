# Earfood – Roy Hargrove

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/earfood-roy-hargrove), 2026-09-28

## Music

- **Earfood** (Roy Hargrove, 2008, album)  
  The Roy Hargrove Quintet album the whole episode is about, a mix of straight-ahead swing, ballads and backbeat grooves. The hosts call it a masterpiece, one of the best entry points to jazz and one of the best-engineered jazz records ever.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Earfood%20Roy%20Hargrove) · 0:14

- **Tenors of Our Time** (Roy Hargrove, 1994, album)  
  Early Hargrove straight-ahead album with several tenor saxophonists. They say no one played straight-ahead jazz better than him then.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Tenors%20of%20Our%20Time%20Roy%20Hargrove) · 5:36

- **Voodoo** (D'Angelo, 2000, album)  
  Neo-soul classic with Hargrove's horn arrangements, which the hosts love and have covered on the show before.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Voodoo%20D%27Angelo) · 7:34

- **Distractions** (The RH Factor, 2006, album)  
  Hargrove's second RH Factor album with D'Angelo. The hosts say it sounds like Voodoo, is "so good" and want to cover it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Distractions%20The%20RH%20Factor) · 10:41

- **Kind of Blue** (Miles Davis, 1959, album)  
  Named as a great-sounding record and as the mountaintop of jazz greatness. They think Earfood is close but not quite as good.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Kind%20of%20Blue%20Miles%20Davis) · 46:00

- **Explorations** (Bill Evans Trio, 1961, album)  
  Named among the best-engineered jazz albums.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Explorations%20Bill%20Evans%20Trio) · 46:16

- **A Night at the Village Vanguard** (Sonny Rollins, 1958, album)  
  Called the best-sounding Village Vanguard recording, even without a piano.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/A%20Night%20at%20the%20Village%20Vanguard%20Sonny%20Rollins) · 46:25

- **Mysterious Traveler** (album)  
  Praised for one of the greatest-sounding piano performances on record.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Mysterious%20Traveler) · 47:03

- **Songbook** (Kenny Garrett, 1997, album)  
  A "great call" for a best-sounding jazz album.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Songbook%20Kenny%20Garrett) · 47:18

- **Maiden Voyage** (Herbie Hancock, 1965, album)  
  Called a great-sounding record, especially for the horns.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Maiden%20Voyage%20Herbie%20Hancock) · 47:49

- **A Love Supreme** (John Coltrane, 1965, album)  
  Praised as a great-sounding Impulse record.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/A%20Love%20Supreme%20John%20Coltrane) · 48:07

- **Crescent** (John Coltrane, 1964, album)  
  Praised along with A Love Supreme as a great-sounding Impulse record.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Crescent%20John%20Coltrane) · 48:12

- **Standards, Vol. 2** (Keith Jarrett Trio, 1985, album)  
  Named as an ECM record that belongs among the best-sounding jazz albums.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Standards%2C%20Vol.%202%20Keith%20Jarrett%20Trio) · 48:34

- **Facing You** (Keith Jarrett, 1971, album)  
  One host puts it in their top five for sound. The other finds the sound weird but still likes the album.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Facing%20You%20Keith%20Jarrett) · 48:38

- **Sarah Vaughan Live at Mr. Kelly's** (Sarah Vaughan, 1958, album)  
  The live record where Hargrove learned Speak Low. They hear its influence directly and call it great.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Sarah%20Vaughan%20Live%20at%20Mr.%20Kelly%27s%20Sarah%20Vaughan) · 58:37

- **Payton's Place** (Nicholas Payton, 2003, album)  
  Suggested as a follow-up to Earfood and called a great one.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Payton%27s%20Place%20Nicholas%20Payton) · 1:10:43

- **Tha Carter III** (Lil Wayne, 2008, album)  
  Another 2008 album the hosts would love to cover on the show.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Tha%20Carter%20III%20Lil%20Wayne) · 1:10:47

- **Fleet Foxes** (Fleet Foxes, 2008, album)  
  Another 2008 album the hosts want to cover, since it is almost fall.
