# Earfood – Roy Hargrove

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/earfood-roy-hargrove), 2026-09-28

## Music

- **Earfood** (Roy Hargrove, 2008, album)  
  Hargrove's quintet album, which the hosts call a 21st-century classic and an ideal entry point for the jazz-curious. They also praise the sound, the playing and the tunes. They discuss I'm Not So Sure (15:08), Brown (23:10), Strasbourg / St. Denis (27:24), Starmaker (32:01), Joy Is Sorrow Unmasked (34:09), The Stinger (38:56), Rouge (44:43), Mr. Clean (49:06), Style (51:37), Divine (54:01), To Wisdom the Prize (55:45), Speak Low (57:11) and Bring It On Home to Me (1:00:26).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Earfood%20Roy%20Hargrove) · 15:08

- **Tenors of Our Time** (Roy Hargrove, 1994, album)  
  Hargrove's straight-ahead album with several star tenor saxophonists. The hosts call it great and say nobody played straight-ahead jazz better than him then.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Tenors%20of%20Our%20Time%20Roy%20Hargrove) · 5:36

- **Voodoo** (D'Angelo, 2000, album)  
  Neo-soul classic with Hargrove on trumpet. The hosts play Spanish Joint (7:39) and say they could connect any topic to it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Voodoo%20D%27Angelo) · 7:34

- **Hard Groove** (The RH Factor, 2003, album)  
  Hargrove's jazz, hip-hop and neo-soul project with guests from the Soulquarian circle. They play Poetry (9:46) and say they want to cover the album on the show.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Hard%20Groove%20The%20RH%20Factor) · 9:42

- **Kind of Blue** (Miles Davis, 1959, album)  
  Named as one of the best-sounding jazz records. They also call it the mountaintop and a gauge for ultimate greatness. Earfood does not quite match it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Kind%20of%20Blue%20Miles%20Davis) · 1:12:17

- **Explorations** (Bill Evans, 1961, album)  
  Named as one of the best-engineered jazz albums of all time.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Explorations%20Bill%20Evans) · 46:16

- **Waltz for Debbie** (Bill Evans, 1962, album)  
  A Bill Evans live record at the Village Vanguard. They call it one of the best-sounding Vanguard recordings.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Waltz%20for%20Debbie%20Bill%20Evans) · 46:21

- **A Night at the Village Vanguard** (Sonny Rollins, 1958, album)  
  Named as the best-sounding Village Vanguard recording, even without a piano.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/A%20Night%20at%20the%20Village%20Vanguard%20Sonny%20Rollins) · 46:27

- **Songbook** (Kenny Garrett, 2004, album)  
  Praised as a great-sounding album when the hosts discuss engineering.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Songbook%20Kenny%20Garrett) · 47:18

- **Maiden Voyage** (Herbie Hancock, 1965, album)  
  Named as a great-sounding record, especially for the horns.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Maiden%20Voyage%20Herbie%20Hancock) · 47:49

- **A Love Supreme** (John Coltrane, 1965, album)  
  Praised as a great-sounding Impulse record.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/A%20Love%20Supreme%20John%20Coltrane) · 48:09

- **Crescent** (John Coltrane, 1964, album)  
  Called "so great" together with the other Impulse Coltrane albums.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Crescent%20John%20Coltrane) · 48:12

- **Facing You** (Keith Jarrett, 1972, album)  
  An ECM album with a weird sound that one host puts in their top five for sound. Both hosts call it great.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Facing%20You%20Keith%20Jarrett) · 48:38

- **Live at Mister Kelly's** (Sarah Vaughan, 1957, album)  
  Sarah Vaughan's live record, which Hargrove loved. The hosts say he learned Speak Low from her version.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Live%20at%20Mister%20Kelly%27s%20Sarah%20Vaughan) · 58:34

- **Payton's Place** (Nicholas Payton, 2003, album)  
  Suggested as a possible future episode, and called a great one.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Payton%27s%20Place%20Nicholas%20Payton) · 1:10:43
