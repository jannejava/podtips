# Love Deluxe - Sade

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/sade-final), 2026-11-02

## Music

- **Love Deluxe** (Sade, 1992, album)  
  Sade's fourth album, praised for its restraint, simple but rich minor harmony, and Sade's phrasing on ninths and elevenths. The hosts find no flaws and rate it highly. Songs discussed: No Ordinary Love (7:37), Feel No Pain (14:52), I Couldn't Love You More (17:15), Like a Tattoo (20:52), Kiss of Life (25:58), Cherish the Day (29:39), Pearls (34:04), Bullet Proof Soul (39:39), Mermaid (41:46).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Love%20Deluxe%20Sade) · 7:27

- **Black Radio** (Robert Glasper Experiment, 2012, album)  
  Contains Lalah Hathaway's cover of Cherish the Day, which the hosts call the best cover of the song. They also praise Casey Benjamin's playing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Black%20Radio%20Robert%20Glasper%20Experiment) · 38:21

- **Sensation** (Sweetback, 1996, song)  
  From the band made by Sade's members without Sade. A hip track that bridges smooth jazz and neo-soul.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Sensation%20Sweetback) · 44:07

- **Automatic for the People** (R.E.M., 1992, album)  
  Named among the great 1992 albums. One host calls it a very good album and says it was the first CD they bought.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Automatic%20for%20the%20People%20R.E.M.) · 46:29

- **Ingénue** (k.d. lang, 1992, album)  
  Called an incredible album in the 1992 roundup.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Ing%C3%A9nue%20k.d.%20lang) · 46:38

- **Here's to Life** (Shirley Horn, 1992, album)  
  Called a great record in the 1992 roundup.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Here%27s%20to%20Life%20Shirley%20Horn) · 46:44

- **The Chronic** (Dr. Dre, 1992, album)  
  Called a great and very influential record, though the hosts rank Love Deluxe above it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Chronic%20Dr.%20Dre) · 46:53
