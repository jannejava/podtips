# The Coziest Tracks We Know

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/sweater-weather), 2026-10-19

## Movies and series

- **Love Jones** (Theodore Witcher, 1997)  
  A Chicago movie with an incredible soundtrack. One of the host's favorite movies.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Love%20Jones%201997) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Love%20Jones) · 17:32

## Music

- **Here's to Life** (Shirley Horn, 1992, album)  
  Jazz vocal album. The hosts open with the title song (1:33) and call it one of their favorites: lush harp and woodwinds, cozy harmonies, like a hot shower after shoveling snow.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Here%27s%20to%20Life%20Shirley%20Horn) · 1:33

- **Come Away With Me** (Norah Jones, 2002, album)  
  Grammy-winning Blue Note album that brought back the cozy jazz sound. Discussed song: Come Away With Me (3:53).  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Come%20Away%20With%20Me%20Norah%20Jones) · 3:11

- **The Nearness of You** (Georgia Heers, song)  
  A young vocalist with guitar, praised for her cozy vibrato, intonation and substitutions. The hosts saw her on Broadway in Good Night, and Good Luck.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Nearness%20of%20You%20Georgia%20Heers) · 4:49

- **Pink Moon** (Nick Drake, 1972, album)  
  Intimate folk album with an incredible voice. Discussed song: Pink Moon (6:38). The hosts agree it is cozy, with a bit of an open-road feel.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Pink%20Moon%20Nick%20Drake) · 6:38

- **With a Flower** (Nicole McCabe & Savannah Harris, album)  
  Saxophone and drums duo record that is surprisingly cozy. The hosts highly recommend it and praise both players.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/With%20a%20Flower%20Nicole%20McCabe%20%26%20Savannah%20Harris) · 9:14

- **Voodoo** (D'Angelo, 2000, album)  
  Called the show's top album. Discussed song: Feel Like Makin' Love (11:12), a cover the host prefers to the original, with cozy voice, Rhodes and guitar.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Voodoo%20D%27Angelo) · 10:44

- **The Closer I Get to You** (Roberta Flack & Donny Hathaway, 1977, song)  
  A quiet storm duet with swirling synth and a cozy bridge harmony. Not the host's favorite track, but the coziest.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Closer%20I%20Get%20to%20You%20Roberta%20Flack%20%26%20Donny%20Hathaway) · 12:55

- **Waltz for Debby** (Bill Evans Trio, 1961, album)  
  Live trio album from the Village Vanguard. Discussed song: My Foolish Heart (14:36), with Scott LaFaro's warm bass sound.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Waltz%20for%20Debby%20Bill%20Evans%20Trio) · 14:36

- **Rendezvous** (Jacky Terrasson & Cassandra Wilson, 1998, album)  
  Blue Note album that introduced the host to Cassandra Wilson. The host was blown away by how she mixed it up with the trio.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Rendezvous%20Jacky%20Terrasson%20%26%20Cassandra%20Wilson) · 17:19

- **Love Jones: The Music (Soundtrack)** (Various artists, 1997, album)  
  Soundtrack praised as one of the best. Discussed song: You Move Me by Cassandra Wilson (17:54), which she wrote herself.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Love%20Jones%3A%20The%20Music%20%28Soundtrack%29%20Various%20artists) · 17:32

- **What'll I Do?** (Nat King Cole, 1953, song)  
  Irving Berlin ballad played by a trio of guitar, bass and piano without drums. Praised for its warmth and romantic tone.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/What%27ll%20I%20Do%3F%20Nat%20King%20Cole) · 19:19

- **By All Means** (Aaron Parks, album)  
  New modern jazz album with Ben Street, Billy Hart and Ben Solomon. Discussed song: Dense Phantasy (21:55), cozy even though it is modern jazz.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/By%20All%20Means%20Aaron%20Parks) · 20:55

- **For Emma, Forever Ago** (Bon Iver, 2007, album)  
  Heartbreak album recorded in a Wisconsin cabin. Discussed song: re: stacks (23:52). Called intimate, cozy and a favorite.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/For%20Emma%2C%20Forever%20Ago%20Bon%20Iver) · 22:40

- **Thriller** (Michael Jackson, 1982, album)  
  The surprise last pick. Discussed song: The Lady in My Life (26:39), which the host first doubted but the hosts agree is cozy.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Thriller%20Michael%20Jackson) · 26:39

- **John Coltrane and Johnny Hartman** (John Coltrane & Johnny Hartman, 1963, album)  
  Named at the end as a whole album that could have been on the cozy list.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/John%20Coltrane%20and%20Johnny%20Hartman%20John%20Coltrane%20%26%20Johnny%20Hartman) · 29:14

- **Ballads** (John Coltrane, 1963, album)  
  Named at the end as another cozy album that was missed.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Ballads%20John%20Coltrane) · 29:18
