# The Coziest Tracks We Know

[You'll Hear It](https://youllhearit.com/episodes/sweater-weather), 2026-10-19

## Music

- **Here's to Life** (Shirley Horn, 1992, song)  
  A lush ballad with harp, woodwinds and keyboard behind Horn's voice. The hosts call it one of their favorite songs and compare it to a hot shower after shoveling snow.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Here%27s%20to%20Life%20Shirley%20Horn) · 1:33

- **Come Away with Me** (Norah Jones, 2002, album)  
  The Grammy-winning Blue Note hit, with the title track played on the show. The hosts say it nailed the cozy sound it was aiming for.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Come%20Away%20with%20Me%20Norah%20Jones) · 3:11

- **The Nearness of You** (Georgia Heers, song)  
  A standard sung with a warm guitar tone, played by Tyreek McDole. The hosts praise the voice, vibrato and substitutions.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Nearness%20of%20You%20Georgia%20Heers) · 4:49

- **Pink Moon** (Nick Drake, 1972, song)  
  An intimate guitar and voice track. The hosts debate whether it is cozy or more "open road", and agree it fits cozy.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Pink%20Moon%20Nick%20Drake) · 6:38

- **With a Flower** (Nicole McCabe and Savannah Harris, album)  
  A saxophone and drums duo record that is surprisingly cozy. The hosts highly recommend it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/With%20a%20Flower%20Nicole%20McCabe%20and%20Savannah%20Harris) · 9:14

- **Feel Like Makin' Love** (D'Angelo, 2000, song)  
  D'Angelo's cover from the album Voodoo, which one host prefers to the Roberta Flack original. They love the voice, Rhodes and guitar.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Feel%20Like%20Makin%27%20Love%20D%27Angelo) · 10:44

- **The Closer I Get to You** (Roberta Flack and Donny Hathaway, 1978, song)  
  A quiet storm duet with swirling synth and a cozy bridge harmony. The host says it is not their favorite track by the pair, but the coziest.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Closer%20I%20Get%20to%20You%20Roberta%20Flack%20and%20Donny%20Hathaway) · 12:55

- **My Foolish Heart** (Bill Evans Trio, 1961, song)  
  From the album Waltz for Debby, with Scott LaFaro's warm bass sound. The hosts say Evans's trio is among the coziest jazz trios.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/My%20Foolish%20Heart%20Bill%20Evans%20Trio) · 14:36

- **Rendezvous** (Jacky Terrasson and Cassandra Wilson, 1998, album)  
  A Blue Note album that introduced one host to Cassandra Wilson. They were blown away by how she mixed it up with the trio.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Rendezvous%20Jacky%20Terrasson%20and%20Cassandra%20Wilson) · 16:14

- **You Move Me** (Cassandra Wilson, 1997, song)  
  Wilson's own song from the Love Jones soundtrack, which the hosts call one of the best soundtracks. They praise her voice and her simple, interesting arrangement.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/You%20Move%20Me%20Cassandra%20Wilson) · 16:14

- **What'll I Do?** (Nat King Cole, 1953, song)  
  An Irving Berlin ballad sung with guitar, bass and piano and no drums. The hosts call Cole possibly the most romantic singer of all time.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/What%27ll%20I%20Do%3F%20Nat%20King%20Cole) · 19:19

- **Dense Phantasy** (Aaron Parks, album)  
  A modern jazz album by Aaron Parks' band By All Means, with Ben Street, Ben Solomon and Billy Hart. The hosts say modern jazz can be cozy too.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Dense%20Phantasy%20Aaron%20Parks) · 20:55

- **For Emma, Forever Ago** (Bon Iver, 2007, album)  
  Recorded in a Wisconsin hunting cabin after a heartbreak. The hosts call the whole album intimate and cozy and play "re: stacks".  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/For%20Emma%2C%20Forever%20Ago%20Bon%20Iver) · 22:40

- **The Lady in My Life** (Michael Jackson, 1982, song)  
  A cozy cut from Thriller. The host hedged at first, then they agree it is cozy, including the drums, horns and harmony.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Lady%20in%20My%20Life%20Michael%20Jackson) · 25:57

- **John Coltrane and Johnny Hartman** (John Coltrane and Johnny Hartman, 1963, album)  
  Named at the end as a whole album that could have been on the cozy list.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/John%20Coltrane%20and%20Johnny%20Hartman%20John%20Coltrane%20and%20Johnny%20Hartman) · 28:57

- **Ballads** (John Coltrane, 1962, album)  
  Named at the end as another cozy album the hosts missed.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Ballads%20John%20Coltrane) · 28:57
