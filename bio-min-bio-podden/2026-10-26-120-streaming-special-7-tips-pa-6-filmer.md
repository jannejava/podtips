# 120. – Streaming Special 7! – Tips på 6 filmer!

[Bio min Bio podden](https://shows.acast.com/bio-min-bio-podden1/episodes/120-streaming-special-7-tips-pa-6-filmer), 2026-10-26

## Movies and series

- **Throw Momma from the Train** (Danny DeVito, 1987)  
  A black comedy where a failed writer and his strange student plan to swap murders, in the style of Hitchcock's Strangers on a Train. The hosts love the title, the script, DeVito's directing debut and Anne Ramsey's performance. Where: Amazon Prime.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Throw%20Momma%20from%20the%20Train%201987) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Throw%20Momma%20from%20the%20Train) · 10:54

- **The Last Boy Scout** (Tony Scott, 1991)  
  A stylish action film with Bruce Willis and Damon Wayans, written by Shane Black, with great one-liners. The hosts like it as a solid, entertaining early 90s action film. Where: Viaplay.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=The%20Last%20Boy%20Scout%201991) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=The%20Last%20Boy%20Scout) · 18:48

- **Something's Gotta Give** (Nancy Meyers, 2003)  
  A romantic comedy for people over 50, with Jack Nicholson, Diane Keaton and Keanu Reeves. The hosts call it warm and charming, like a blanket on the sofa, with a sharp script. Where: Netflix.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Something%27s%20Gotta%20Give%202003) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Something%27s%20Gotta%20Give) · 33:52

- **Paddington 2** (Paul King, 2017)  
  The bear Paddington is sent to prison after a thief steals a pop-up book. The hosts call it the perfect family film, with a great cast and Hugh Grant in his best role. Where: Netflix.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Paddington%202%202017) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Paddington%202) · 39:46

- **Unstoppable** (Tony Scott, 2010)  
  Denzel Washington and Chris Pine try to stop a runaway freight train. The hosts see it as an underrated guilty-pleasure thriller, made exciting by Tony Scott's craft. Where: Disney+.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Unstoppable%202010) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Unstoppable) · 49:16

- **Blow Out** (Brian De Palma, 1981)  
  A sound technician played by John Travolta records what proves to be a murder disguised as a car accident. The hosts like it as a political thriller with a clever ending, and note it is one of Tarantino's favourites. Where: Amazon Prime.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Blow%20Out%201981) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Blow%20Out) · 54:13
