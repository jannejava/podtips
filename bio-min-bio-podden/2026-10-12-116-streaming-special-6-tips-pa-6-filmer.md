# 116. – Streaming special 6! – Tips på 6 filmer!

[Bio min Bio podden](https://shows.acast.com/bio-min-bio-podden1/episodes/116-streaming-special-6-tips-pa-6-filmer), 2026-10-12

## Movies and series

- **Source Code** (Duncan Jones, 2011)  
  A sci-fi thriller where a soldier relives the last eight minutes of a train bomb victim to find the bomber. The hosts like it as a tight, smart and well-made popcorn film. Where: Viaplay.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Source%20Code%202011) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Source%20Code) · 2:35

- **The Judge** (David Dobkin, 2014)  
  A courtroom and father-son drama with Robert Downey Jr. as a cynical lawyer defending his father, played by Robert Duvall. The host recommends it strongly, and it is a tip that the other host has not seen yet. Where: Netflix.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=The%20Judge%202014) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=The%20Judge) · 12:22

- **Prey** (Dan Trachtenberg, 2022)  
  A Predator prequel set in 1719 with a Comanche woman who hunts the alien. The hosts say it is the best Predator sequel and recommend it with all their heart. Where: Disney+.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Prey%202022) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Prey) · 18:08

- **Världens värsta människa (The Worst Person in the World)** (Joachim Trier, 2021)  
  A smart, human romcom about a woman near 30 who questions her career, love and life. The hosts praise the style and Renate Reinsve, who won a prize in Cannes for it. Where: Netflix.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=V%C3%A4rldens%20v%C3%A4rsta%20m%C3%A4nniska%20%28The%20Worst%20Person%20in%20the%20World%29%202021) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=V%C3%A4rldens%20v%C3%A4rsta%20m%C3%A4nniska%20%28The%20Worst%20Person%20in%20the%20World%29) · 30:18

- **The Taking of Pelham One Two Three** (Joseph Sargent, 1974)  
  A 70s thriller where four men hijack a New York subway car and ask for a million dollars. The hosts call it the best hostage film, with Walter Matthau and Robert Shaw, and say the remakes are not as good. Where: Prime.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=The%20Taking%20of%20Pelham%20One%20Two%20Three%201974) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=The%20Taking%20of%20Pelham%20One%20Two%20Three) · 34:37

- **Copycat** (Jon Amiel, 1995)  
  A dark 90s thriller where a psychologist (Sigourney Weaver) and a detective (Holly Hunter) hunt a killer who copies famous serial killers. The host loves it as a good evening film that stands out because of its two female leads. Where: Amazon Prime.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Copycat%201995) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Copycat) · 47:44
