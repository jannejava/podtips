# 119. – The Social Reckoning, Tenzing och Master and Commander

[Bio min Bio podden](https://shows.acast.com/bio-min-bio-podden1/episodes/119-the-social-reckoning-tenzing-och-master-and-commander), 2026-10-23

## Movies and series

- **Foul Play** (Colin Higgins, 1978)  
  Comedy thriller with Goldie Hawn and Chevy Chase. One host has started watching it and loves Goldie Hawn's charm and timing. Where: SVT Play.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Foul%20Play%201978) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Foul%20Play) · 20:44

- **The Adventures of Tintin** (Steven Spielberg, 2011)  
  Animated adventure that the hosts adore, with the motorcycle chase as a highlight. They are thrilled that Peter Jackson will make a sequel.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=The%20Adventures%20of%20Tintin%202011) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=The%20Adventures%20of%20Tintin) · 22:02

- **The Social Reckoning** (Aaron Sorkin, 2026)  
  Sorkin's follow-up to The Social Network about the Facebook whistleblower Frances Haugen. Great script and cast, but cold direction; both hosts give it 3.5 to 4 and recommend it. Where: in cinemas.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=The%20Social%20Reckoning%202026) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=The%20Social%20Reckoning) · 27:04

- **Tenzing** (Jennifer Peedom)  
  The hosts were not impressed. Biopic about Tenzing Norgay and the 1953 Everest expedition. Beautiful and well acted but slow and sleepy; both hosts give it 2.5 and say skip it. Where: Apple TV+.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Tenzing) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Tenzing) · 52:15

- **Master and Commander: The Far Side of the World** (Peter Weir, 2003)  
  Napoleonic naval adventure with Russell Crowe and Paul Bettany. Anders's favorite of the week and an underrated epic with great detail and a warm friendship. Where: Disney+.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=Master%20and%20Commander%3A%20The%20Far%20Side%20of%20the%20World%202003) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=Master%20and%20Commander%3A%20The%20Far%20Side%20of%20the%20World) · 1:16:18

- **A Fish Called Wanda** (Charles Crichton, 1988)  
  Edward's favorite pick for next week: a heist comedy with John Cleese, Jamie Lee Curtis, Kevin Kline and Michael Palin. He watched it a hundred times when young.  
  [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/find/?q=A%20Fish%20Called%20Wanda%201988) · [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/search?search=A%20Fish%20Called%20Wanda) · 1:36:55
