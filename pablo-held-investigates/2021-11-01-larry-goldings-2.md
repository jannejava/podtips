# Larry Goldings 2

[Pablo Held Investigates](https://pabloheldinvestigates.com/larry-goldings-again/), 2021-11-01

## Music

- **Mompou album (Arcadi Volodos plays Mompou)** (Arcadi Volodos, 2007, album)  
  Larry says Volodos made a beautiful Mompou album with a big sound and virtuosic arrangements.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Mompou%20album%20%28Arcadi%20Volodos%20plays%20Mompou%29%20Arcadi%20Volodos) · 6:35

- **Hand Jive** (John Scofield, 1994, album)  
  Larry calls it an important record for him. His family played it in the car.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Hand%20Jive%20John%20Scofield) · 9:04

- **Brahms: Symphony No. 4** (Johannes Brahms, album)  
  Pablo says Brahms is heavy and modern. He loves the blues feel and the spiralling downward melody.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Brahms%3A%20Symphony%20No.%204%20Johannes%20Brahms) · 11:07

- **Switched-On Brandenburgs** (Wendy Carlos, 1979, album)  
  Bach's Brandenburg concertos played on Moog. They praise how expressive it is and say it shows the quality of Bach's music.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Switched-On%20Brandenburgs%20Wendy%20Carlos) · 12:44

- **Snowflakes Are Dancing** (Isao Tomita, 1974, album)  
  Synthesizer reworks of Debussy. Larry says they are incredible and bring out new sides of the music.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Snowflakes%20Are%20Dancing%20Isao%20Tomita) · 14:54

- **Rachmaninoff: Piano Concertos** (Vladimir Ashkenazy, album)  
  Larry has vivid memories of this recording. He loves the orchestration and Ashkenazy conducting from the piano.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Rachmaninoff%3A%20Piano%20Concertos%20Vladimir%20Ashkenazy) · 16:21

- **Romeo and Juliet** (Sergei Prokofiev, album)  
  The lovers' theme with pizzicato strings flipped Larry out. He analyzes its harmony in detail.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Romeo%20and%20Juliet%20Sergei%20Prokofiev) · 19:10

- **Scintillation** (Carlos Salzedo, song)  
  A harp piece Pablo has played on repeat for months. They listen to it together and love its chords.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Scintillation%20Carlos%20Salzedo) · 1:09:17

- **Sonatas and Interludes for Prepared Piano** (John Cage, 1948, album)  
  Pablo plays the first piece. He loves the idea of writing for a remade instrument.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Sonatas%20and%20Interludes%20for%20Prepared%20Piano%20John%20Cage) · 1:15:42

- **The Complete Vee-Jay Recordings** (Billy Preston, album)  
  Includes a gospel record Preston made at 20, with organ and piano overdubs. Larry says it was a huge turnaround for him.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Complete%20Vee-Jay%20Recordings%20Billy%20Preston) · 2:01:07

- **Notes with Attachments** (Blake Mills & Pino Palladino, 2021, album)  
  Larry played on this record and describes the process with Blake Mills. He praises Mills's way of shaping the music.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Notes%20with%20Attachments%20Blake%20Mills%20%26%20Pino%20Palladino) · 2:11:15

- **Violin Concerto** (Erich Wolfgang Korngold, album)  
  Larry has Itzhak Perlman recordings of it. He calls the composer's work beautiful and great.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Violin%20Concerto%20Erich%20Wolfgang%20Korngold) · 2:12:58

- **Harp Concerto** (John Williams, album)  
  Larry saw the score at Tanglewood. Pablo listened to it afterwards and says it is beautiful.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Harp%20Concerto%20John%20Williams) · 2:13:59

- **The Complete "Is" Sessions** (Chick Corea, album)  
  Larry's favorite Chick Corea record, with Dave Holland and others.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Complete%20%22Is%22%20Sessions%20Chick%20Corea) · 2:26:04

- **Autumn Leaves** (Chick Corea & Bobby McFerrin, song)  
  A funny duo version. Corea's playing behind McFerrin feels like magic.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Autumn%20Leaves%20Chick%20Corea%20%26%20Bobby%20McFerrin) · 2:29:49

- **Filles de Kilimanjaro** (Miles Davis, 1968, album)  
  Larry loves Chick Corea on some of its tracks.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Filles%20de%20Kilimanjaro%20Miles%20Davis) · 2:34:10

- **Light as a Feather** (Return to Forever, 1973, album)  
  Larry listened to it a lot and loved the Rhodes playing.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Light%20as%20a%20Feather%20Return%20to%20Forever) · 2:34:47
