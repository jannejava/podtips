# John Patitucci

[Pablo Held Investigates](https://pabloheldinvestigates.com/john-patitucci/), 2022-12-14

## Music

- **Phantom Navigator** (Wayne Shorter, 1987, album)  
  Wayne Shorter album with Patitucci on Forbidden Planet. The host says he loves it, while Patitucci feels the production did not do the music justice.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Phantom%20Navigator%20Wayne%20Shorter) · 24:11

- **Footprints Live!** (Wayne Shorter, 2002, album)  
  Live album of the Shorter quartet. The host first heard Atlantis here and kept replaying it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Footprints%20Live%21%20Wayne%20Shorter) · 36:31

- **Directions in Music** (Herbie Hancock, Michael Brecker, Roy Hargrove, 2002, album)  
  Live tribute to Miles Davis and John Coltrane, recorded at Massey Hall. The host names it one of his favorite albums, and Patitucci praises Hancock's comping on it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Directions%20in%20Music%20Herbie%20Hancock%2C%20Michael%20Brecker%2C%20Roy%20Hargrove) · 58:02

- **Live in Italy** (John Patitucci, album)  
  Patitucci's first live album, a trio with Chris Potter and Brian Blade. The host calls it a beautiful record.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Live%20in%20Italy%20John%20Patitucci) · 1:04:16

- **Remembrance** (John Patitucci, 2009, album)  
  Trio album with Brian Blade and two saxophonists, Chris Potter and Joe Lovano. The host says it is one of his favorites.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Remembrance%20John%20Patitucci) · 1:05:14

- **Play Scarlatti** (Enrico Pieranunzi, album)  
  Pieranunzi plays Scarlatti sonatas as written, then adds a meditation on each. Patitucci says it is totally killing and his favorite Pieranunzi.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Play%20Scarlatti%20Enrico%20Pieranunzi) · 1:52:13

- **The Soul of the Bass** (John Patitucci, album)  
  Patitucci's solo bass album, recorded at the Bunker studios in Brooklyn. The host's favorite song on it is Seeds of Change.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Soul%20of%20the%20Bass%20John%20Patitucci) · 1:58:02

- **One More Angel** (John Patitucci, 1997, album)  
  Chamber jazz album with Paul Motian, recorded without rehearsal. The host loves it.  
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/One%20More%20Angel%20John%20Patitucci) · 2:24:42
